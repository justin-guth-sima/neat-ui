Add a camera

A stream starts from a camera on the board's network. The developer docs list the cameras that have been tried.

Before you start

  • The camera's address, such as rtsp://192.168.1.20/stream1.
  • Its user name and password, if it has them.

Add it

  1. Open Media and choose Add stream.
  2. Paste the address, or pick the camera from the list:
    • a network camera shows its address;
    • a USB camera shows its device, /dev/video0.
  3. Press Enter to add it, or Esc to leave.

A stream keeps its name when the camera's address changes.

When nothing shows

Check that the camera answers on its port, then reload the page with Ctrl R.
